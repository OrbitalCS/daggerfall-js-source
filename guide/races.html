Races

Eight races, and none of them changes your attributes or skills: those come from your class. What a race does is narrower than its screen suggests, and this page sets the two side by side.

The eight races:

BretonRedguardNordDark ElfHigh ElfWood ElfKhajiitArgonian

Reading the entries

  • Weapon bonuses are added to your damage and your chance to hit with that kind of weapon, and only for you, never for an enemy of that race. Under DFO's default combat (the Physical Combat and Armor Overhaul) they are built from your attributes, so the entries give the numbers with every attribute at 50 and at 75. Under Daggerfall Unity's rules, offline with the overhaul off, three races get a bonus that grows with your level instead.
  • A resistance adds 30 to your saving throw against spells of that kind: from 55% to 85% with Willpower 50. An immunity makes the throw certain.
  • Heat and cold: with survival on, as it is by default, each race carries a little warmth of its own or lacks it. Bretons and Nords feel the cold later and the heat sooner; Redguards, Dark Elves and Khajiit the other way round; Argonians twice as much as those; High and Wood Elves neither.
  • Townsfolk: the people walking the Iliac Bay's streets are only ever Bretons, Redguards and Nords.
  • Looks (2026-10-25): with your own Morrowind data attached, characters wear Morrowind's 3D bodies and heads; DFO's own rebuilt heads exist so far only for Breton men played by players. Without it, every race uses Daggerfall's original art.

Breton

Resists Magic (+30 save) · Handles cold better, heat worse · No weapon bonus

Homeland
High Rock. Bretons walk the Iliac Bay's streets.
The race screen says
A people steeped in magic, harder to harm with it than any other, and so gifted in every arcane art.
Weapon bonuses
None, with the combat overhaul on or off.
Resistances and immunities
Resists Magic: +30 to the saving throw.
Movement and survival
With survival on, feels the cold a little later and the heat a little sooner. Climbs and swims like anyone.
In play
Hostile Magic spells land on you less often, and the cold bites a little less. Nothing in the game makes a Breton's own spells stronger, cheaper or more likely to succeed.

Redguard

Weapon bonus with long blades, axes, blunt weapons and bows · Handles heat better, cold worse

Homeland
Hammerfell. Redguards walk the Iliac Bay's streets.
The race screen says
A hardy, quick people, born with a gift for weapons that makes them fine warriors.

  • Long Blade: damage from Agility, Strength; to hit from Agility, Speed, Luck.
  • Axe: damage from Strength, Agility; to hit from Strength, Agility, Luck.
  • Blunt Weapon: damage from Strength, Endurance; to hit from Strength, Agility, Luck.
  • Archery: damage from Strength, Agility; to hit from Agility, Speed, Luck.

Offline with the combat overhaul turned off: level/3 to damage and to hit with every weapon but a bow.

Resistances and immunities
None.
Movement and survival
With survival on, feels the heat a little later and the cold a little sooner. Climbs and swims like anyone.
In play
The widest weapon race in the game, with a bonus in four weapon skills. "Hardy and quick" is flavour: no formula gives a Redguard more health or speed.

High Elf

Immune to Paralysis · No weapon bonus

Homeland
Sumurset Isle. High Elves never walk the Iliac Bay's streets.
The race screen says
Clever, agile and willful natural spellcasters, untouched by paralysing poison and magic.
Weapon bonuses
None, with the combat overhaul on or off.
Resistances and immunities
Immune to Paralysis: the saving throw is certain.
Movement and survival
With survival on, feels heat and cold exactly as the weather and clothes make them. Climbs and swims like anyone.
In play
A paralysing spell never takes hold of you, which can decide a fight. "Natural spellcasters" has nothing behind it: no formula gives a High Elf more spell points or cheaper or stronger spells.

The race screen says
A hardy, clever, agile people, fine climbers, and adept at every thief's art.

Argonian

Weapon bonus with short blades · No swimming fatigue, never drowns in the open world · Handles heat much better, cold much worse

Homeland
Black Marsh. Argonians never walk the Iliac Bay's streets.
The race screen says
A clever, agile, quick reptilian people who do not tire swimming, with thieves, mages and some warriors among them.
Weapon bonuses

Default rules:

At 50 / at 75 in every attribute
WeaponDamageTo hit
Short Blade+2 / +4+5 / +8
  • Short Blade: damage from Agility, Speed; to hit from Agility, Speed, Luck.

Offline with the combat overhaul turned off: no weapon bonus.

Resistances and immunities
None.
Movement and survival
Never pays the extra fatigue other races risk each minute in water. With the Iliac Puddle No More mod's open sea, on by default, never drowns in the open world. With survival on, feels the heat noticeably later and the cold noticeably sooner: the coldest-blooded race.
In play
Water holds no danger, which the screen undersells. Its thieves and mages get nothing from race: the one combat bonus is the short blade.
