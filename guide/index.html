How to play

What DFO Online is

DFO Online is The Elder Scrolls II: Daggerfall, rebuilt to run in a web browser, with online play. It runs on a desktop or a phone, and there is nothing to install. It started as a faithful copy of Daggerfall Unity and is now its own game: most of it plays as Daggerfall does, and where it differs, these pages say so.

It is not finished, and it changes every day.

Get your game data in

DFO Online does not come with Daggerfall's own files. You bring them, once. Daggerfall is free from any of these:

  • Steam
  • GOG
  • DaggerfallGameFiles.zip, the game files on their own

On a desktop: press Play and pick your ARENA2 folder, or drop it on the page. On Steam it is under DF/DAGGER/ARENA2.

On a phone: a phone browser cannot be handed a folder, so hand over the zip itself. Put DaggerfallGameFiles.zip on the phone (AirDrop it on an iPhone), press Play, and choose the zip. Do not unzip it first.

The game keeps what you gave it, so that browser will not ask again. Your saves live in the browser's storage too. The desktop app keeps them as real files instead, and reads ARENA2 straight from your Daggerfall install.

Make your first character

Character creation runs in Daggerfall's order:

  1. Race and gender

    Races says what each race really gets.

  2. Class

    Answer Daggerfall's questions and be given one, pick one of its eighteen from the list, or build your own. Custom classes explains the builder.

  3. Biography

    Answer its questions, or let the game write it for you.

  4. Name and face

    Name your character and pick a face.

  5. Attributes and skills

    Spend your bonus points. Attributes and Skills say what each one really does in this game.

  6. Reflexes

    Higher reflexes make enemies attack sooner and land their extra strikes more often. In return, every use of a skill counts for more: a quarter more at Very High, a quarter less at Very Low.

If you play online, or have turned on the Oblivion Remastered leveling mod, you are also asked how to level. Online it is always Daggerfall's way: your primary, major and minor skills add up to a sum, and every 15 points of it is a level.

Your first hour

  1. You wake in Privateer's Hold

    As everyone does: a dungeon, and the way out is the first thing to find. Pick up what you can carry.

  2. Controls

    Daggerfall Unity's defaults: WASD and the mouse, F6 for your pack, Esc for the rest. Touch controls appear on their own on a phone. You can rebind anything in Settings.

  3. Use your skills

    A skill rises from use, and the game checks for a raise at most once every six game hours, so resting is when you see it go up.

  4. Save often

    F9 quick saves and F11 quick loads.

Coming from Daggerfall Unity?

Most of DFO plays as Daggerfall Unity does. The differences that change a fight or a build the most:

  • Dodging counts half, not a quarter, against an enemy's chance to hit you. Dodging
  • Critical strikes multiply damage, up to double at skill 100, and Luck makes them come more often. In Daggerfall Unity they only help you hit. Critical Strike
  • Agility counts a quarter of the gap to hit, Speed an eighth, and Luck also defends you. In Daggerfall Unity Agility counts a tenth and Speed nothing. Agility, Speed, Luck
  • Race weapon bonuses come from your attributes, not your level, and six races have them, not three. Races
  • Skills can pass 100, up to 200, through Master Skills: always on online, a switch offline. Master Skills
  • Climbing is a whole moveset, with ledges, mantles, vaults and leaps, when Enhanced Climbing is on, as it always is online. Climbing
  • Online, Endurance counts back. Your health never falls below what your current Endurance would have given at every level. Endurance
